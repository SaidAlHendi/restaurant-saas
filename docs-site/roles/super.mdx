title: "Super admin"
description: "Full platform control, including suspending and reactivating restaurants."
icon: "shield-check"
---

## Who this role is for

The Sahtain platform owner or a trusted operator who administers the whole platform. It is the highest admin role and includes everything billing and support can do.

## What super admins can do

- Everything the **billing** and **support** roles can do.
- Suspend a restaurant, which blocks its staff from signing in.
- Reactivate a suspended restaurant.
- Every suspend and reactivate action is written to the audit log.

## What super admins cannot do

- Act as a restaurant user or change a restaurant's orders and menu from the admin app. Admin access is separate from restaurant roles.

## Where they work

The admin app, with the full sidebar. The restaurant detail page shows the suspend and reactivate action only to this role.

<Note>
  Admin roles are checked against the database on every request, so a demotion or deactivation takes effect immediately.
</Note>
