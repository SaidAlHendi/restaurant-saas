## Who this role is for

Front-of-house staff who take customer orders and handle payment at the counter.

## What cashiers can do

- Open the POS, build an order from the menu, and send it to the kitchen.
- Change a cart item's options before sending. Select the pencil icon on the item in the cart to reopen its options (for example size or extras), with the current choices already ticked. Change them, the quantity or the note, then select **Save changes**.
- Take payment, following the restaurant's pay-before-send and card settings. When the owner has set a display currency, the POS shows prices, the order total, the cash received and the change in that currency, with the total already rounded. Collect the total shown. A warning appears at the top of the POS when the exchange rate is older than 7 days; tell the owner or a manager.
- Enter a **Pager no.** on an order when the branch uses pickup numbers (see below).
- Run dine-in tables from the **Tables** page: one open bill per table visit, several rounds of orders, pay by guest or item, and move an order to another table (see below).
- View orders, including the POS orders page and the open-orders strip.
- Update order status. This also gives them access to the kitchen display.
- View the menu and the branches they are assigned to.
- Sign in with a PIN on a linked device, and lock the screen between customers. On the lock screen, pick your name and enter your PIN to switch user, or sign out.

## Pager numbers and the Ready screen

When the owner turns on **Pickup numbers / pagers** for a branch, the cart shows an optional **Pager no.** field. Type the number of the pager you hand to the customer (1 to 9999) before you send the order.

- The pager number shows on the kitchen display card and on kitchen tickets and customer receipts.
- When the kitchen marks the order **Ready**, its number appears on the branch's customer **Ready** screen, with a chime. Orders without a pager show the order number.
- The field is optional. Leave it empty for orders that have no pager.

## Tables and table bills

Open **Tables** in the sidebar. Every table shows as a card: **free**, **busy** (an open bill) or **settled** (everything paid, bill still open). A busy card shows the number of rounds, the amount still due and any pager numbers.

- **New order** on a free table opens the POS with that table already selected.
- **Add round** on a busy table does the same. When the guests order again, the new order joins the table's open bill as the next round. Busy tables stay selectable in the POS.
- If two separate groups share one table, choose **New separate bill** so each group pays on its own.
- **Bill** opens the bill: all rounds and items. Label items with a **Guest** number if guests pay separately.
- **Pay selected** takes payment for the ticked items only. Pay with cash (change and the display currency are shown) or card. A bill can be paid in several steps, and an item cannot be paid twice.
- Takeaway orders have no bill and work as before.
- **Close table** is available once every order is completed and nothing is due. A settled bill closes by itself when a new order arrives at that table.
- An order that is both ready and fully paid is completed automatically, whichever happens last (kitchen marks it ready, or the cashier takes the payment).

### Moving an order to another table

If an order went to the wrong table (for example T4 instead of T5), open the bill and use **Move table** to move the whole order, or **Move** on a single round. Choose the new table. The order joins that table's open bill, or starts a new one, and an empty old bill is closed.

- Only dine-in orders that are not cancelled or refunded can be moved.
- An order that already has a payment cannot be moved.
- Moving a single item to another table is not available yet.

## What cashiers cannot do

- Edit the menu, branches, staff or settings.
- See the dashboard or reports. Opening a dashboard address sends them back to the Cashier screen.
- See billing.
- Cancel an order that has been placed, or void items on an order that is preparing or ready, without approval. A manager or owner must enter their PIN on the approval dialog. Cancelling a draft order needs no approval.

<Note>
  Five wrong PIN attempts lock the account for five minutes.
</Note>

## Signing in with a PIN

PIN-only staff have no email or password. A manager or owner links the device once (**Devices** page, using a pairing code that works for 15 minutes). After that, the sign-in page on that device opens straight to the **choose your name and enter your PIN** screen and lists only the staff of that branch. Signing out returns to the same PIN screen. Removing the device on the **Devices** page ends its access.

## Where they work

The restaurant app. The sidebar shows only **Cashier** and **Kitchen**; the menu is available inside the POS. After sign-in, a cashier lands on the **Cashier** screen.
