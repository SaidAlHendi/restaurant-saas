## Who this role is for

Chefs and kitchen staff who prepare orders and need a live view of what to cook.

## What kitchen staff can do

- See incoming orders on the kitchen display in real time.
- Tick items off as they are cooked. A new order shows as **New**. Tap **Start** on it to move it to **Preparing**, so the other cooks and the cashier can see it is being cooked. Ticking its first item also starts it by itself.
- Tap **Ready** once to mark the whole order ready, even on a **New** order that has no ticks yet. A 5-second **Undo** message lets you take it back. The order returns to **Preparing**, and items you had already ticked stay ticked; the rest go back to being cooked.
- View order details, including items and notes.
- Spot repeat orders: when a table orders again, the new ticket shows **Round 2** (or 3, and so on) next to the table, so the kitchen knows it belongs to a table already being served. The first round shows no label. The printed kitchen slip does not show the round.

## What kitchen staff cannot do

- Hand orders over to customers: the cashier completes a ready order with **Picked up** or **Served**.
- Create orders or take payment.
- Cancel or void orders.
- View or edit the menu, branches, staff, settings, reports or billing.

## Where they work

The restaurant app's **Kitchen** screen. After sign-in, kitchen staff land there, and a dashboard address sends them back to it. It is the only item in their sidebar.
