## Who this role is for

A trusted team member who runs the restaurant day to day, such as a shift lead or general manager.

## What managers can do

- Create, view and cancel orders, and update order status.
- Edit the menu: categories, products, modifier groups (choices such as size or extras, with optional price changes) and ingredients.
- Choose which branches show a product or category. A manager with access to every branch can pick **All branches** or specific branches. A manager limited to some branches can only pick from their own branches, and can hide an item from their branches without changing the others.
- See the dashboard and reports.
- View the staff list on the **Staff** page. A manager limited to some branches sees only staff who share at least one of their branches (plus themselves and staff with access to every branch). Use the search box (name or email) and the role filter to find people; the **Branches** column shows the branch names, or a count when someone works in more than two. Managers can also invite new staff, and edit **cashier** and **kitchen** accounts: change their role between those two, set branch access, create PIN-only accounts, and disable them.
- Link and remove POS devices on the **Devices** page, so PIN-only staff can sign in on a shared tablet.
- Approve cashier requests to cancel or void an order, by entering their PIN.

## What managers cannot do

- Manage billing or the subscription.
- Edit restaurant settings, or open the **Branches** page to add or change branches and their tax rates.
- Create, edit, promote or disable owners or other managers.
- Change their own role, status or branch access.
- Access the platform admin app.

## Where they work

The restaurant app: **Today**, **Orders**, **Menu**, **Cashier**, **Kitchen**, **Staff** and **Devices**. There is no Branches, Settings or Billing item, because those are for owners.
