---

## Who this role is for

Support staff who help restaurants and need to look up their account, without changing anything.

## What support admins can do

- View the platform overview.
- Search and filter the list of restaurants by name and status.
- Open a restaurant's detail page.

## What support admins cannot do

- Suspend or reactivate restaurants (super only).
- Manage subscriptions or plans (billing and above).
- Change any restaurant data.

<Note>
  Users, audit log and support tools are listed in the admin sidebar but are not built yet.
</Note>

## Where they work

The admin app, on the **Overview** and **Restaurants** pages.
