## Who this role is for

The restaurant's owner or business operator, who is accountable for the whole restaurant. Every restaurant has at least one owner, and the last active owner cannot be demoted or disabled.

## What owners can do

- Create, view and cancel orders, and update order status.
- View and edit the menu: categories, products, modifier groups and ingredients. A modifier group is a question for the customer, such as "Size" (must pick 1) or "Extras" (pick up to 2), and its modifiers are the answers, each with an optional price change. Create groups under **Menu → Modifier groups**, or select **New group** inside a product's form, then tick the group on every product that offers it. Each product and category is shown in **All branches** (the default, which also covers branches added later) or only in the branches you choose, in its form on the **Menu** page.
- See the dashboard and reports: today's sales, order history and trends.
- Manage branches on the **Branches** page: add a branch, rename it, turn it on or off, and edit its settings. Branch settings cover the address, tax rate, whether prices include tax, cashier payment options, pickup numbers and pagers, the business day start, the timezone and the printed receipt. Adding a branch is subject to the plan's branch limit.
- Manage staff on the **Staff** page: invite staff, create PIN-only staff accounts, change roles, restrict staff to specific branches, and disable accounts. Owners can assign any role, including other owners.

- Fine-tune one employee's access with **Permissions** in the Staff row actions: grant a permission their role lacks (for example, rotating table QR codes or reopening completed orders) or deny one it has. Only owners can do this, and owner accounts cannot be customised. Changes apply on the employee's next action. See [Roles overview](/roles/overview#per-employee-permission-overrides).

- Link and remove POS devices on the **Devices** page, so PIN-only staff can sign in on a shared tablet without an owner session. A device is linked with a pairing code that works once for 15 minutes.
- Edit restaurant settings such as the name, logo, languages and display currency in **Settings**.
- Manage the subscription: view the plan, billing status and invoices.
- Approve cashier requests to cancel or void an order, with their PIN.

## Pickup numbers and the customer Ready screen

In a branch's settings, under **Payments**, turn on **Pickup numbers / pagers**. It is off by default and needs the Restaurant plan or higher.

Once on, cashiers get an optional **Pager no.** field on each order, and the number shows on kitchen tickets and receipts. Under the switch, Sahtain shows the address of the branch's customer **Ready** screen, in the form `/display/<restaurant-slug>/<branch-slug>`. Open it on a TV or tablet facing customers. It needs no login, lists the numbers of orders that are ready (an order stays listed until the cashier marks it **Picked up** or **Served**), and plays a chime when a new one appears (select **Turn on sound** once, because browsers block audio until a tap). If the switch is off, or the plan no longer includes the feature, the screen shows as unavailable.

## Display currency and exchange rate

Prices are always stored in the restaurant's base currency. In **Settings**, under **Currency**, you can choose a different **display currency** and type the **exchange rate** yourself (how many units of the display currency equal 1 unit of the base currency).

The base currency can be changed in the same section until the restaurant takes its first order. Price numbers stay as typed, so 20 stays 20 in the new currency, and every branch follows. After the first order it is locked, because existing orders and reports are saved in it.

Once a display currency is set:

- The public menu, the POS, the kitchen display and receipts show prices in the display currency only.
- For currencies with large numbers, such as SYP, LBP and IQD, item prices are rounded to the nearest 100 and order totals to the nearest 500 by default. Other currencies start with no rounding. You can change both rounding steps at any time.
- Each order keeps the rate it was created with. Changing the rate later affects the menu and new orders, never past orders.
- The dashboard and daily reports show amounts in the display currency, using each order's own rate. Billing for the Sahtain subscription is not affected.
- **Settings** shows when the rate was last updated, and the POS shows a warning when the rate is older than 7 days.

To stop converting, set the display currency back to the base currency. Orders taken before a display currency was set have no stored rate, so order lists show them in the base currency.

## What owners cannot do

- Remove or demote the last active owner.
- Change their own role, status or branch access. Another owner must do that.
- Access the platform admin app or other restaurants' data.
- Create orders when the restaurant's subscription blocks new orders. The restaurant can still view history and manage settings.

## Where they work

The restaurant app, with every sidebar item available: **Today**, **Orders**, **Menu**, **Cashier**, **Kitchen**, **Branches**, **Staff**, **Devices**, **Billing** and **Settings**.
