## Who this role is for

Platform finance or account staff who manage restaurants' subscriptions and payments.

## What billing admins can do

- Everything the **support** role can do: browse restaurants and view the platform overview.
- Work on subscriptions, plans, payments, invoices and revenue.

## What billing admins cannot do

- Suspend or reactivate restaurants. Only **super** can.

<Warning>
  The billing screens (subscriptions, plans, payments, invoices, revenue) are not built yet and show a coming-soon page. No endpoint currently requires the billing role specifically, so today this role has the same access as support.
</Warning>

## Where they work

The admin app.
