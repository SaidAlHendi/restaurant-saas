Only the **owner** has the Billing page. Payments are handled by Paddle, and you manage your card and invoices from the **Manage subscription** button.

## Free trial

- Every new restaurant starts a 14-day trial on the **Restaurant** plan. No card is needed.
- A banner appears for owners in the last 3 days of the trial.
- When the trial ends, the restaurant moves to the **Free** plan. It is not suspended, and nothing is deleted. Features outside the Free plan are locked, and creating new items above Free limits is blocked until you subscribe.

## Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Billing** in the dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose a plan and select **Subscribe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Complete payment in the Paddle checkout. Your plan activates when Paddle confirms the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you already have an active subscription, use **Switch to this plan** instead. A second checkout is refused, so you are never billed twice.

<Info>
  If online payment is not set up for your environment, the Billing page tells you to contact support to change your plan.
</Info>

## Change plan

Select **Switch to this plan** on another plan. The change takes effect immediately and the price difference is prorated.

## Cancel

Select **Cancel and move to Free**. You keep your paid plan until the end of the period you already paid for, and the Billing page shows **Ends on** with the date. Select **Keep my plan** before that date to undo the cancellation.

## If a payment fails

The Billing page shows **Your last payment failed**. Your plan stays active while Paddle retries the payment. Update your card in **Manage subscription** to fix it.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Trial | Inside the 14-day trial. |
| Active | Paid and current. |
| Payment failed | Last charge failed. Plan stays active while Paddle retries. |
| Trial ended | Trial finished without a subscription. You are on Free. |
| Canceled | Subscription canceled. Paid plan lasts until the period end, then Free. |

## Platform-granted plans

A restaurant can be given a complimentary plan with no payment provider, which is how demo restaurants are set up. It shows as **Active** and is not billed. There is no admin screen for granting these yet.

See [Plans and pricing](/subscriptions/plans) for what each plan includes.
