Sahtain has four plans, billed monthly in USD. Only the restaurant **owner** can view or change the plan, from **Billing** in the dashboard.

|  | Free | Menu | Restaurant | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | \$0 | \$12 / month | \$39 / branch / month | \$79 / branch / month |
| Products | Up to 40 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Staff per branch | 1 | 1 | 10 | 10 |
| Devices per branch | n/a | n/a | 4 | 4 |
| Menu in Arabic and English | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Your logo, no "Powered by" | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Custom menu look (colours, layout, banner, cover photo, brand colour, search, ticker, closing line) | No, default look | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| QR code per table (menu) | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Cashier (POS) | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Kitchen display | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Daily reports | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Advanced reports and export | No | No | No | Yes |
| Multiple branches | No | No | No | Yes |
| Custom roles | No | No | No | Yes |
| Ordering from table QR | No | No | No | Yes |
| API access | No | No | No | Yes |

<Note>
  Restaurant and Business are priced per branch. The monthly charge is the plan price times your number of active branches. Menu and Free are a flat price.
</Note>

## Menu look and your plan

Free restaurants use the default menu look (Classic colours, list layout). From the **Menu** plan up, owners can change it in **Settings → Menu look**; the trial counts as the Restaurant plan, so it includes this. On Free the page is read-only and shows which plan unlocks it.

If you move down to Free, Sahtain keeps your saved look and cover photo but shows guests the default look. If you upgrade again, your look comes back without you doing anything, usually within a few minutes.

## What happens at a limit

When you reach a plan limit, Sahtain blocks **new** items only. Nothing is deleted or hidden. For example, a Free restaurant with 40 products cannot add a 41st, but keeps all 40. A feature your plan does not include shows an upgrade screen.
